These patterns can help HPD and DOT choose areas for coordinated local review. They do not establish that housing causes crashes or identify any development as dangerous.

How to read this responsibly

Housing counts follow project start years and include preservation, rather than all newly occupied homes. SIRTA contains investigated serious crashes, not every NYC crash. Counts are not adjusted for population or traffic exposure. Missing values stay unknown. ZIP data are incomplete and selected; the matched ZIP view excludes ambiguous geography and 2022. 2026 stops in June and is excluded by default.

Housing and crash category filters apply independently to each source. A housing filter does not mean the selected crashes occurred at those buildings. Research correlations are descriptive and include repeated places over time.
